======================================================================
  [1] ETF flow: institutional demand (read the 5d/30d sums, never one day)
  [2] Premium: US vs global bid (+ = US leads, - = US is the missing bid; 20d+ streak = regime)
  [3] 30d mean net of Tether: is the US discount a regime or just noise?
  [4] Stables: money in/out of crypto (7d AND 30d up = capital returning)
  [5] Venue flow: net spot taker buying per exchange. Only Binance+OKX publish history;
        Coinbase is a live snapshot (context only) and Bybit has none. Verdict uses the deep books.
  [6] Positioning: Binance futures L/S ratios - CONTRARIAN. High ratio = crowd long =
        squeeze fuel DOWN; low = crowd short = squeeze fuel UP. Top-trader pos falling =
        smart money exiting longs. OI draining = liquidation, not new shorts. Mid-range = no signal.
  [7] Funding/basis: cost of leverage. Positive/longs-pay = crowded longs (bearish tilt);
        negative/shorts-pay = crowded shorts (squeeze fuel UP). Basis = perp premium to index.
  [8] Fear & Greed: crowd panic/greed 0-100. <20 = capitulation (bounce fuel UP, bad to short);
        >80 = euphoria (contrarian-bearish). Shows where the crowd sits, not where price goes.
  [9] Bitfinex margin longs: hourly margin-long size (ONE aggregate book, no account detail)
        (each hour it grew, weighted by that hour's price) = the average price of the adds, i.e.
        the bid. Spot below it means those adds are underwater and it is supply overhead. The
        block also prints how past surges of this size resolved against a random-hour baseline.
  [10] URPD: supply by cost basis (bars above spot = resistance, at/below = support)
  [11] Floors: 1.0x Balanced = where past cycle bottoms landed; AVWAP = market break-even
  [12] MVRV: market value / realised value. 1.00 = market at break-even; below 1.00 the average
        holder is under water, which only happens near bottoms. The floor multiple rises each
        cycle, so the dollar floor is a ZONE (two fits printed, they disagree by design). The
        clock starts at the first sub-1.00 print - the low lands 26-149 days later, not on it.
  [13] RSI (daily): momentum 0-100 (>70 overbought, <30 oversold; fast>slow = uptrend intact)
  [14] Divergence: 20d low + HIGHER RSI14 (lows >=5d apart). 9y test: plain BULLISH has NO
        edge (55% vs 53% baseline); BULLISH with RSI14<35 at the 2nd low = 67% win vs 53%.
